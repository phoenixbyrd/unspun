Alex: Why does the Strait of Hormuz matter so much?

Jordan: It is the narrow waterway between Iran and Oman, and the world's most important oil chokepoint. Roughly a fifth of the world's petroleum consumption passes through it. Reporting puts tanker transit roughly ninety percent below pre-war levels, and one analysis puts the disruption at twenty to twenty-five percent of global seaborne crude and liquefied natural gas. One note: the president said quote massive amounts of oil are coming out of the Hormuz Strait, end quote. The shipping data cited in the coverage does not support that. Transit is severely reduced.
